Post-processing

The eigenfunctions returned by the solver are Gridap finite element functions (CellField). Every observable that can be written as an integral over the mesh is computed with the same quadrature used to build the Hamiltonian. The functions below act on a vector of eigenfunctions ϕ and return one value per state. U is the trial space, Ω the triangulation and dΩ the measure (see Low-level interface).

orthogonality_check(ϕ, dΩ)``

The eigenvectors returned by ARPACK are normalized, and eigenvectors of different eigenvalues are orthogonal. Within a degenerate subspace (e.g. the $(n+1)$-fold levels of the 2D isotropic oscillator) the complex ARPACK driver returns linearly independent vectors that are not necessarily orthogonal. If an orthonormal basis of the subspace is needed, orthonormalize it, e.g. with Gram–Schmidt using sum(∫(ϕ[j]' * ϕ[i]) * dΩ) as inner product.

Known issue in v0.1.0: normalization of reduced densities

reduced_density returns $(1-\Delta x/L)\,\rho^{(1)}$ instead of $\rho^{(1)}$ ($\Delta x$ is the spacing of the sampling points and $L$ the length of the domain). The shape is correct: divide by the integral of the returned values to normalize.

Information-theoretic quantities

FunctionComputes
time_indep_entropy(ϕ, U, dΩ)Shannon entropy $S=-\int\rho\log_2\rho\,d\Omega$ (bits)
time_indep_entropy(ϕ, U, dΩ, α)Rényi entropy $S_\alpha=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\log_2\int\rho^\alpha d\Omega$ — see the known issue below
reduced_time_indep_entropy(r, ρ)for a density $\rho(x_i, y_j)$ sampled on a grid: $-\int\rho(x_i,y)\log_2\rho(x_i,y)\,dy$ for every $x_i$, and the analogous quantity for every $y_j$ (trapezoidal rule)
time_indep_diff_mutual_information(Sx, Sy, Sxy)$I = S_x + S_y - S_{xy}$

To obtain the mutual information between the two coordinates of a 2D state, compute the entropies of the reduced densities $\rho^{(1)}$, $\rho^{(2)}$ (one number each) and the total entropy $S$, then $I = S^{(1)} + S^{(2)} - S$. For two particles, $I$ measures the correlation between them; it vanishes for product states.

Known issue in v0.1.0: Rényi entropy

time_indep_entropy(ϕ, U, dΩ, α) evaluates $\rho^{\alpha/\ln 2}$ instead of $\rho^\alpha$. Until it is fixed, compute the Rényi entropy directly: log2(sum(∫((z -> z^α) ∘ ρ) * dΩ)) / (1 - α) with ρ = density(ϕ)[n]. The Shannon entropy is correct.

Writing your own observables

Any integral can be written with Gridap syntax. For example the expectation value of the potential and the kinetic energy of state n:

ψ = ϕ[n]
V_mean = real(sum(∫(ψ' * q * ψ) * dΩ))
T_mean = real(sum(∫(p * (∇(ψ)' ⋅ ∇(ψ))) * dΩ))

and the overlap of two states computed on the same mesh, sum(∫(ψ1' * ψ2) * dΩ).
